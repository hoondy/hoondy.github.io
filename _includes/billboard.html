{%- comment -%} Digital ad panel. {% include billboard.html format="horizontal|rectangle|vertical" variant="large" %} The AdSense unit sits on top of a house-ad layer (promos for the apps). In production site.js requests an ad once the panel is visible; the promos appear only once AdSense reports no fill (or never answers). Sizes are fixed per format and container width (see main.css) so nothing shifts. The panel stays in English on translated pages. bb_count numbers the panels on a page so neighbours open on different promos; a layout that adds panels after the page content sets it from the panels in content.
