hoondy.com ("this site", also called Hoondy Sq) is a personal website run by Hoondy.com, LLC. This page explains what information this site and the services it uses collect when you visit, and what you can do about it. If anything here is unclear, email [admin@hoondy.com](mailto:admin@hoondy.com).

## The short version

- There are no accounts and no sign-ups. You never have to give this site your name or email.
- This site shows ads from Google AdSense and measures traffic with Google Analytics. Both use cookies. Visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent first.
- If you post a score to a game's monthly high score board, the nickname you pick and your score are public for that month.
- The LQ Test has no leaderboard. If you leave its box ticked, your result is added to anonymous statistics with no name and nothing that identifies you.
- Settings such as the dark theme are kept in your own browser.

## Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Google shows a consent message the first time you arrive, whichever page you land on. Your choice there decides whether Google may use cookies for personalized ads and whether Google Analytics may set its cookies. Until you choose, Google Analytics runs with its cookies turned off, and without your consent you see non-personalized or limited ads only. ## Analytics (Google Analytics 4)

This site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages are read and how people find them. It collects things like the pages you view, your approximate location (city or country), your device and browser type, and the page that referred you. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. You can opt out with the [Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

## Comments (Disqus)

Older blog posts have a comment section hosted by Disqus. Nothing from Disqus loads until you press **Show comments**. After that, Disqus's own [privacy policy](https://disqus.com/privacy-policy/) applies to anything you do in the comment box.

## Monthly high score boards

Rush Hour keeps a monthly high score board. Posting a score is optional. When you do, the leaderboard service stores:

- the nickname you type (pick anything; please don't use your full real name if you'd rather stay anonymous),
- your score and a few game details shown on the board (for example the stage you reached),
- the country your connection comes from, derived from your IP address and shown as a flag,
- a salted, one-way hash of your network address, used to limit spam and to keep one entry per player each month. It is cleared once the month is over. The IP address itself is never stored.

Boards reset at midnight New York time on the 1st of each month. Entries on the board are public, and old boards are deleted after two years. To have an entry removed sooner, email [admin@hoondy.com](mailto:admin@hoondy.com) with the nickname, game and month.

## LQ Test statistics

The LQ Test doesn't rank anyone. Instead, it can add your result to anonymous statistics that show everyone where they land (the score distribution on the results page) and that will be used to recalibrate the test's norms.

- **What is sent:** when a valid result is shown, your answers, the language you took the test in, the test version and how long it took. Results flagged as invalid are never sent, and each completed test is sent at most once.
- **What is stored:** the date (day only), the language, the test version, your answers, and the score and leadership type computed from them. The time it took is checked and not stored. There is no name, no IP address, no hash of your IP address, no country and no time of day in these records.
- **Spam limits:** to stop one connection from flooding the statistics, the service counts recent submissions per salted hash of your network address, kept apart from the results and deleted by a daily cleanup.
- **How to opt out:** untick "Add my anonymous result to the LQ statistics" on the test's start page, and nothing is sent. Your browser remembers that choice. Because the stored records can't be traced back to anyone, a single result can't be looked up or removed later.

## Stored in your browser

This site uses your browser's local storage (not cookies) for a few conveniences: your light or dark theme choice, the nickname you last used, your personal best scores, whether you want your LQ results counted, which LQ results were already added, and in-progress app state. It never leaves your device except as described above. Clearing your browser's site data removes all of it.

- **Hosting** is provided by GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs. The leaderboard and statistics service runs on Cloudflare.
